Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version: SavvyMe sets no advertising cookies and does not sell or share your information for ads. Without an account your progress stays on your device. With an account we keep your email, your name and your progress so your streak follows you.

What we collect

  • Without an account. Your quiz answers, course plan, streak and lesson progress are kept in your own browser's storage. They do not leave your device.
  • With an account. Your email address, the name and @handle you pick, and your learning progress (quiz answers, course, streak, Sparks, lessons done, weekly goal and plan). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google, nothing else.
  • Tips and news by email (only if you ask for them). If you tick "Email me tips and news", we keep your email, name, the date you joined and a tag for your course and plan. Our emails count whether they were opened and which links were clicked (a tiny image and links that pass through our server), so we know what is useful. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works in one click, and you can untick the box in the app at any time.
  • Server logs. Like any website, our server briefly records requests (address, time, page) to keep the service running and safe.

We do not sell any of this, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not record your screen.

Who processes it

  • Accounts and progress are stored on our own server in the United States, with a nightly backup kept for 14 days.
  • Signing in with Google or an email code goes through the shared Amaete account, so one account works across Amaete apps.
  • Our tips and news emails are kept and sent by the same shared Amaete service, under SavvyMe's name. It never adds you to any other app's list.
  • Lessons open from our other Amaete lesson sites, and the home page loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Those services see the usual request details (such as your IP address and browser), as any website does.
  • If we add payments, the payment provider handles your card. We never see or store card numbers.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and progress until you ask us to delete them. Backups roll off after 14 days.

Your choices

  • Use SavvyMe without an account and nothing is stored on our server.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to hello@savvymeapp.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • If you live in the EU, the UK or another place with data protection law, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

You must be 13 or older to make an account (16 in EU countries that set a higher age). Younger learners should use SavvyMe on a parent's device or account. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has made an account, write to hello@savvymeapp.com and we will delete it.

Changes
